# Customer discovery questionnaire — vendors, service businesses & logistics

**Purpose:** Capture operational pain points, tooling gaps, and feature priorities from **vendors (retail/wholesale)**, **service businesses**, and **logistics / delivery providers** so product decisions reflect real workflows.

**How to use this document:** Copy sections into your form builder (Google Forms, Typeform, Microsoft Forms, etc.). Where noted, use **Likert scales** (e.g. 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree), **multiple choice**, **checkboxes**, or **short text**.

**Suggested front matter (paste at top of form):**

- Estimated time: **12–20 minutes**
- Anonymous vs identified: [choose one]
- Optional: company name, role, contact if follow-up interviews are offered

## Notes for the product team (internal — do not paste into respondent form)

- **Triangulation:** Compare answers across **vendor vs service vs logistics**; hybrid businesses may answer multiple deep sections.
- **Inventory depth:** F-series + B9 + E2 often surface whether “inventory” is catalog accuracy, physical stock, or both.
- **Autobiz alignment:** Map responses to: conversational commerce, specialist agents (product/payment/logistics/complaint/upsell), central coordination, Paystack-style flows, party state / inbox, analytics, low-stock APIs.
- **Follow-up:** Use B11, K3, K4, L1–L2 to script customer interviews and validate prototypes.
